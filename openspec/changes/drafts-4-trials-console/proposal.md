# Drafts, step 4: Trials in the console

## Why
From a version on a branch, the operator tries it on chosen bindings, follows the trials, and compares each with what is placed before promoting the branch. Step 4 of four. Proposal: [Drafts](../../../Proposals/Drafts.md).

## What Changes
- From a branch's version, the console applies the branch to chosen bindings, the presentations its impact lists offered first, and makes trial jobs of the recipes.
- Each trial is compared with the placed presentation: decisions stream by stream, and the two files to play.
