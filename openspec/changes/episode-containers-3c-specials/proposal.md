# A special placed among episodes is an episode container

## Why
With episodes held as containers, a special placed in a season's run should be one too, and a
special that is another version of an episode should be an alternative of that episode's container
rather than of its season. Proposal:
[An episode is a container of its own](../../../Proposals/EpisodeDetails.md), step 3, the third of its
three pull requests.

## What Changes
- A special placed in a season's run is an episode container among its siblings, drafted as an
  episode is.
- A special placed as a version of an episode is an alternative of that episode's container, in a
  sequence of the container's own.
