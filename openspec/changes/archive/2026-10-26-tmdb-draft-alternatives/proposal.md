# Alternatives drafted, and edited before they are made

## Why
An alternative a special made was a side effect of that special's placement: the special named a
title, and the alternative played it alone, or played it in place of one item of a sequence. Nothing
could be done to it after that. A second special could not be put in it, its entries could not be
moved, and an item could not be dropped from it, so an unaired pilot run of two episodes, or a
colourised serial TMDb lists as several specials, could not be one alternative. Building an alternative
is editing an ordered list, and the list belonged to no special. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- An alternative is drafted for a container in its own right: a title and what it plays, in order,
  each one of the container's items by reference or a special placed in it. It is drafted playing one
  special, or nothing yet, or cloned from one of the container's sequences.
- A special is placed in a drafted alternative at a position or in place of one of its entries. The
  draft's entries can be moved and taken out, and the draft retitled or discarded. A special replaced,
  taken out, or in a discarded draft is unassigned again.
- Placing a special as a new edition, or as a version of an item, drafts the alternative that placement
  made, so a suggestion taken can be edited too. A walk or draft kept with either is read with it drafted.
- Making or storing a container makes each drafted alternative that plays something as a sequence and
  an alternative naming it. Changing a drafted alternative makes its stop pending.
