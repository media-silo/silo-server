# Rulesets in the console, step 3: The console changes them

## Why
The operator changes a ruleset by editing its document as text, checked by the silo as they type, and
stores it only after seeing how it differs from its base and which placed presentations it would make
differently; to the standard, to a branch, or to a new branch for a trial; and promotes a branch with
its impact beside it. A library's standard is named from the console. Step 3 of three.
Proposal: [Rulesets in the console](../../../Proposals/Rulesets.md).

## What Changes
- Drafts: a change from a version, a new ruleset, a return to an earlier version; checked as typed.
- The difference and impact before the store; the store with its base; a 409 keeps the draft.
- Branches started, stored on and promoted, with a promotion's impact and refusal.
- A library's standard named or cleared from the settings.
