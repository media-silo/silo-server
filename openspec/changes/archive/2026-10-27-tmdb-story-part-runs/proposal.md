# A story's part titled otherwise, numbered on, is one of its parts

## Why
A story's parts were found by the story each TMDb title names. TMDb titles some stories' parts under
more than one name: Invasion of the Dinosaurs' first part is `Invasion (1)`, and the rest are `Invasion
of the Dinosaurs (2)` and on. The first part was left out of the run, as a story of its own of one part.
Its serial was then offered to be titled by its numbers without it, and the offer was worded by counting
the run from one, `Episode 1 to 5`, though it would title parts 2 to 6. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A part numbered one after the part before it continues that part's run, though TMDb titles its story
  otherwise. The run is titled by the story most of its parts name, the later on a tie. A story whose
  numbers start again is still a story of its own.
- The offer to title a run by its numbers is worded by the first and last of those numbers.
