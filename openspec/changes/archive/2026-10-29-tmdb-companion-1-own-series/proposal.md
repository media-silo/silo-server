# A companion series of the operator's own as a stop of the walk

## Why
A new companion series was only ever a document the walk made for a special placed in it: it never
appeared in the walk's tree, so nothing could be placed in it, drafted for it or added beneath it as
at the walked series, and it could not be added before a special needed it. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- Beneath the walk's tree, a companion series of the operator's own is added, titled and listed or
  not, holding nothing yet.
- It is a new stop with no stop above it, listed after the root's tree, opened, shaped and confirmed
  as any new stop is; a new companion series made for a special as it is placed is one too.
- Storing creates a confirmed companion series, with what is beneath it, first, at the top of the
  library, holding the entries placed in it from other stops after its own. One not confirmed makes
  nothing, nor do the specials placed in it from elsewhere.
- Taken out of the walk, it leaves with every stop beneath it, and what was placed in it is
  unassigned again.
- A walk kept before companion series were stops reads each it made as a confirmed stop.
