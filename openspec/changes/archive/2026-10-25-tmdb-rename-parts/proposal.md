# A serial's own parts titled, not grouped again

## Why
A serial grouped from the parts TMDb titles as one story, opened in its turn, had the same parts
suggested as a group again, titled as the serial already is. Proposal: [A held tree reviewed against
TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A run of the story a container is titled as, or that is every entry it holds, is not suggested as a
  group: its parts are suggested to be titled `Episode 1` and on, or `Part 1` and on.
- A held container's missing episodes of its own story are not suggested as a group of them.
