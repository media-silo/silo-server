# A drafted alternative cloned, and each alternative's actions in one menu

## Why
A stop's Children list is its container's first sequence, but the Alternatives box listed that
sequence again to offer Clone. A drafted alternative could not be cloned, so a variant of one built
already, such as the pilot run without its coda, had to be built again from the start. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- The first sequence is cloned from an actions menu under the Children heading, and the Alternatives
  box lists only the container's other sequences.
- A drafted alternative is cloned too, from its own actions menu with Rename and Discard. The clone
  plays the original's specials as well: each is an entry of the first drafted alternative that plays
  it, and the rest refer to that entry.
- A special taken out of one drafted alternative stays placed while another still plays it. Placing it
  anywhere else takes it out of every one.
