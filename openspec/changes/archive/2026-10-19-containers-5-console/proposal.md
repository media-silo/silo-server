# Containers in the console

## Why
The console shows nothing of what a library holds. It gains a library browser, a container editor
whose edits are drafts kept on the Mac, and artwork. Proposal:
[Containers in the console](../../../Proposals/Containers.md).

## What Changes
- Each library opens to its tree of containers, and a container shows its sidecar, its presentations,
  its artwork and the last walk's findings on it.
- A container's own fields are edited in a form, as a draft kept on the Mac, checked by the silo's dry
  run, and stored on the sidecar the draft was based on.
- A container's artwork is shown, put and removed.
