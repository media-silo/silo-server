# A series is drafted with every season, and trimmed in its walk

## Why
Picking a series from Wikidata read its seasons at once, to offer them to be chosen before the draft
was made. The walk the draft opens in already lists each drafted season at the series' stop, ticked,
and a season unticked there is left out of the walk and is not made, so choosing seasons before the
draft did again what the walk does, and read the series for it whether or not anything was drafted.

## What Changes
- A series drafted from Wikidata holds a season for each of Wikidata's seasons of it; a season not
  wanted is left out in the walk, at the series' stop.
- Picking a result in the search reads nothing more of it; drafting reads it.
- Choosing a season of a series the library holds, to draft beneath it, is unchanged.
