# A record the library lacks walked from a draft of it

## Why
A record the library held and one it lacked were reviewed in two different sheets: a walk of a held
tree, with the tree, the open stop and the series' specials side by side, confirmed a stop at a time
and stored at the end; and a draft from TMDb, shaped in a sheet of its own with the specials beneath
it, checked as a dry run and made in one go. Both shape containers drafted from TMDb and place a
series' specials, and the walk already makes new containers from drafts, so the operator met the same
work laid out and finished two ways. Proposal: [A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- Picking a record the library lacks starts a walk rooted at the draft's top, a new stop with no stop
  above it, in the walk's sheet; the draft's own sheet and its dry run are gone.
- Storing makes a confirmed root at the top of the library, or, for a film in a collection the
  library holds, names it in the collection and makes it beneath; a drafted series then declines
  the specials declined in it.
- A walk of a record is kept as any walk is, and picking the record again offers to resume it.
- A season picked of a held series opens the series' walk with the season chosen among its missing
  children, rather than drafting it on its own.
