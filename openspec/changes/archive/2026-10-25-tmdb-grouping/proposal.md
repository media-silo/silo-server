# Groups of a draft's entries

## Why
A draft's entries were ticked to be grouped into a container beneath it, which read as choosing them
to be kept, and a group once made could only be dropped whole. TMDb titles a classic Doctor Who
season's episodes as the parts of its serials, `Terror of the Zygons (1)` and on, so the serials a
season holds can be suggested rather than typed. Proposal: [A held tree reviewed against
TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A draft's entries are selected, then made a new group of a kind the operator names, or added to a
  group made already.
- A group is selected whole; its entries are shown in it, each only to be taken out of it, and the
  group can be ungrouped, putting its entries back in its place.
- Runs of entries TMDb titles as `Story (1)`, `Story (2)` and on are bracketed and suggested as a
  serial titled by the story, one at a time or all at once.
- A group ungrouped takes its stop out of a walk, and the specials placed in it are unassigned again.
