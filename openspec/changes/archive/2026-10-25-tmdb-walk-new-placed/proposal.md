# A new stop shows the specials placed in it

## Why
A special placed in a new stop left the unassigned specials and was kept with the walk, but only a held
stop listed the specials placed in it, so one placed in a new stop showed nowhere, and had no way back
among the unassigned from where it was placed. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A new stop lists the specials placed in it as a held stop does, each with how it was placed and a
  way to unassign it.
