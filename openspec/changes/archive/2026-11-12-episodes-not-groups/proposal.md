# A drafted episode container is an entry, not a group

## Why
Since a drafted season holds each episode as a container of its own, the form a new stop's children
are shaped in showed every episode as a group of one, its feature its only member, and ungrouping one
put the feature in the season in its place, the episode's container and title gone. The suggestions
of serials from Wikidata's titles, and of a serial's parts titled by their numbers, found no episodes
held as containers, so none were made.

## What Changes
- An episode container is shown as one of its container's entries, ticked, selected, grouped and
  moved as an episode held as an entry is, and never as a group; the kind a container's groups mostly
  are counts no episode.
- Runs of episode containers Wikidata titles as the parts of one story are suggested as serials, and
  a serial's parts titled by their numbers retitle the episode containers.
- An episode container grouped into a serial and ungrouped again keeps its container and its title.
