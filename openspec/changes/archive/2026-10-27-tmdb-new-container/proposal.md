# A new container added beneath one, from its primary sequence's menu

## Why
A container beneath one could be made only out of entries already there, as a group, or from what TMDb
has, as a missing season. Nothing let the operator add one TMDb knows nothing of, such as a collection
for a series' extras, to fill with specials or entries afterwards. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- The primary sequence's menu offers New Container, with a submenu of the kinds a container may be. The
  container is titled as it is added and holds nothing yet. Its primary sequence goes by the id of the
  one it is beneath.
- In a draft, or a new stop, it is named last in the primary sequence and made with the draft. Entries
  are added to it, and it is removed, as a group's are.
- Beneath a held stop it is a new stop of the walk, ticked there among what was added. Specials are
  placed in it and its children shaped at its own stop. It is made beneath the held container when the
  walk is stored, once both are confirmed. Unticked, it leaves the walk once the held stop is confirmed.
