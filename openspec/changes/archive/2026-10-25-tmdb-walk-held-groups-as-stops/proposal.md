# A held stop's groups are new stops of the walk

## Why
A group made of a held stop's missing episodes was made only when the walk was stored: confirming the
stop added the containers it held and the seasons chosen to the walk's tree, and none of its groups,
so a new serial grouped there had no stop of its own to place its specials in or shape its parts at,
though a draft's groups each have one. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- Confirming a held stop drafts each group it takes as a new stop beneath it, shaped and given
  specials there as a draft's groups are, drafted again when its episodes change, and out of the walk
  once it is ungrouped or unticked and the stop confirmed again.
- Storing names it where its first episode would have gone, as before, and makes it from its draft
  once its own stop is confirmed.
