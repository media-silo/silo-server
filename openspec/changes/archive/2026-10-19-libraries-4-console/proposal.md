# Libraries, step 4: The console's libraries

## Why
SiloAdmin shows each library's availability and last walk, and adds and removes libraries within the roots. Step 4 of four. Proposal: [Libraries](../../../Proposals/Libraries.md).

## What Changes
- The console shows libraries, their availability and their last walk.
- It adds libraries within the roots and removes them, naming the job that holds one.
