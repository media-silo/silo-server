# This Mac's settings say what a new container is ticked with

## Why
A new container in a walk is ticked with everything it is offered, so an operator who never wants
one kind of field, TMDb's outlines, which carry TMDb's attribution and must be read again once its
terms no longer let them be kept, has to untick it at every new stop, episode by episode. TMDb's token
and language sat in the console's sidebar, which belongs to a silo, though they are this Mac's.

## What Changes
- SiloAdmin has a Settings window for what is this Mac's, for every silo: TMDb's token, language and
  notice, moved from the sidebar, and the fields a new container is ticked with to begin with.
- The fields are Wikidata's year, first date, cast, directors and writers, and references to providers
  other than Wikidata, and TMDb's outline and each kind of its artwork, all ticked to begin with.
- A new stop is ticked with every field it is offered but those the settings leave unticked, whenever
  it joins a walk; a stop made before the settings changed keeps its ticks. A new container's Wikidata
  reference is always ticked, and a held stop is still ticked with nothing.
