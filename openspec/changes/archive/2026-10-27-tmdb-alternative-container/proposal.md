# A new container played in a drafted alternative alone

## Why
A drafted alternative could play the container's items and specials, and nothing else. A cut that
holds a run of its own, such as an omnibus collection the broadcast order lacks, had nowhere to be: a
container added beneath the container was named in its primary sequence, so every other way of
playing it held the container too. Proposal: [A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A drafted alternative's menu offers New Container, with a submenu of the kinds a container may be.
  The container is titled as it is added, holds nothing yet, and is played in the alternative alone.
- In a draft it is a container of the draft, beneath the alternative's container. In a walk it is a new
  stop: beneath a held stop, one not listed among its children since only the alternative plays it, or,
  beneath a new stop, a container of its
  draft and a stop once that one is confirmed.
- Making the alternative names the container in its own sequence, and none of the container's own
  sequences names it. A clone of the alternative refers to it.
- Taken out of every alternative that plays it, the container leaves the draft or the walk.
