# Several specials in one alternative

## Why
A special placed as another version of a container, or of one of its items, made an alternative of its
own, and nothing let a second special join it. Placing a second as another version with the same title
made a second alternative. An entry could go only into a sequence the container already held, so a
sequence a placement makes was not offered. Had it been, the order TMDb lists the specials in would
have decided whether the sequence existed yet when the entry was placed. An unaired pilot run of two
episodes, or a colourised serial TMDb lists as several specials, could not be one alternative. Proposal:
[A held tree reviewed against TMDb](../../../Proposals/TmdbWalk.md).

## What Changes
- A special may join an alternative of the container it is placed in, one the container holds or one
  another special's placement makes, either last in its sequence or in the place of an item the
  sequence refers to.
- Specials joining an alternative are placed after those that make one, whatever TMDb's order.
- A special that made an alternative and is placed so it makes none in that container returns the
  specials joining it to the unassigned.
