# Confirm and everything beneath is in Confirm's menu

## Why
A stop's *Confirm* and *Confirm with everything beneath* stood side by side as two buttons, the second
too long for the space beside the stop's title and often cut short.

## What Changes
- A stop's *Confirm* is one button, with *Confirm and everything beneath* in its menu; once the stop
  is confirmed the button's own action is *Unconfirm*, and the menu still offers to confirm everything
  beneath. The action is named *Confirm and everything beneath*.
