# Rulesets in the console, step 2: The console reads the rulesets

## Why
For a silo it holds a passkey for, the console shows each ruleset, its branches and versions, the
document of any version as stored beside the silo's reading of it, and each library's out-of-date
presentations from the background check — drawing everything from the silo and parsing nothing
itself. Step 2 of three. Proposal: [Rulesets in the console](../../../Proposals/Rulesets.md).

## What Changes
- A Rulesets section: the rulesets, branches and versions, each version's document and reading, a
  scope with no catch-all marked, and what each version made.
- Each library's out-of-date presentations, and how many are still to be checked.
